Direct package-local commands are nonauthorizing static self-checks. They cannot authenticate the `validate-static.mjs` bytes they execute. A later authenticated lane or other outside-package caller must raw-pin and hash `validate-static.mjs` before import or execution, then supply an independently pinned review anchor.

The sealed production lifecycle requires the caller to supply all four anchor-pin fields. The package does not discover, store, or derive them:

node validate-static.mjs \
  --mode sealed \
  --review-anchor-root "$GB0EAC_REVIEW_ANCHOR_ROOT" \
  --review-anchor-locator "$GB0EAC_REVIEW_ANCHOR_LOCATOR" \
  --review-anchor-bytes "$GB0EAC_REVIEW_ANCHOR_BYTES" \
  --review-anchor-raw-sha256 "$GB0EAC_REVIEW_ANCHOR_RAW_SHA256"
