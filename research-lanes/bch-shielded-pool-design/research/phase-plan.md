# PoolActionFv1 Circle-FRI research phase plan

This plan keeps the product relation fixed while evidence narrows the
cryptographic design. A phase may eliminate a candidate early; it may not
promote a component result into complete-transaction evidence.

## Authority and model routing

- Root owns the relation, threat interpretation, evidence ledger, integration,
  phase gates, candidate selection, fallback authorization, and promotion.
- SOL reviews architecture/security at P0, after decisive P2/P3 evidence, before
  selecting parameters, and before promotion. SOL does not replace measurement.
- Terra implements complex approved designs: reference arithmetic, semantic
  oracle, BCH transaction builder, verifier kernels, AIR, prover, and CLI.
- Luna handles bounded mechanical work: schemas, codecs after their ABI is
  frozen, vectors, thin harness adapters, benchmark execution, result
  normalization, reproducibility, and documentation.
- Worker write scopes are disjoint. A worker may report alternatives or a
  contradiction but may not alter the product relation, 128-bit target,
  fallback rule, qualification gates, or promotion status.

Every worker prompt includes the defensive research context, exact input files,
an explicit write scope, one measurable output, reproduction commands, source
and artifact hashes, assumptions, a pass/fail gate, and stop conditions.

   never mislabeled as complete evidence.

- Deposit and withdrawal shells include state, carriers, external input,
  payout/change, tokens, source outputs, and exact wire overhead.
- The remaining proof-byte envelope is exact for every topology.
- Any topology requiring replenishment, an extra action transaction, ignored
  context, or impossible 128-bit proof room is killed and retained in evidence.

## P3 — soundness, ZK, and candidate selection

For each surviving concrete tuple, first create an additive provenance-closed
composition and soundness schema revision. The current v2 files cannot qualify
anything. Then complete:

- a qualification worksheet which mechanically resolves source commits,
  candidate/proof artifacts, measurements, raw transactions, and standardness
  evidence (v1 is legacy; prequalification v2 cannot support selection);
- `security/zk-leakage-review.schema.json`;
- canonical encoding and parser evidence;
- exact query/proof/transaction byte derivation; and
- PQ/classical component inventory.

Root and SOL select only a measured non-dominated tuple. Security gates dominate
bytes and speed. No 100–127-bit tuple is selectable while
`fallbackAuthorized=false`.

## P4 — approved complete implementation

Terra implements the selected reference semantics, optimized arithmetic,
trace generator, AIR, masked prover, verifier, carrier scripts, transaction
builder, and desktop CLI. Differential tests compare reference and optimized
paths. Planck optimization is admitted only with equivalence and adversarial
regression evidence.

Exit gate:

- One proof per deposit and withdrawal for the exact frozen relation.
- No generic VM, Stwo, Cairo, coordinator, remote prover, batching, transfer,
  variable amount, or runtime selector enters the implementation.

## P5 — complete qualification

Required evidence for both actions:

- complete serialized transaction and every source output;
- at most 100,000 bytes under the pinned current standard rules;
- current unmodified BCHN and Libauth acceptance;
- per-input VM/op/hash/stack/carrier budgets and LeanBCH agreement;
- valid corpus plus all semantic, transaction, topology, parser, proof, ZK,
  recovery, and liveness mutations;
- cold/warm median, p95, maximum, failure rate, peak process-tree RSS, effective
  cores/affinity, and complete withdrawal preparation under 60 seconds; and
- exact commits, dirty state, commands, parameters, logs, and artifact hashes.

Zero-confirmation node/mempool acceptance is the operational submission
boundary. Confirmation waiting is not part of the CLI success gate.

## P6 — conditional fallback

Closed. Root/SOL may open it only after complete optimized 128-bit deposit and
withdrawal transactions are both measured and fail the 100,000-byte envelope.
The fallback then selects the strongest concretely derived fitting tuple at or
above 100 bits; it is not a free parameter reduction.

## Global stop conditions

Stop the affected track and report when:

- a result changes the frozen product relation;
- proof/signature construction becomes circular;
- state, token, carrier, payout, fee, or context substitution is possible;
- nullifier nonmembership or unique insertion is absent;
- a ZK construction leaks or lacks a defensible argument;
- soundness accounting is heuristic or omits a composed term;
- provenance is dirty, unpinned, mismatched, or fabricated;
- component bytes are presented as a complete transaction;
- a benchmark silently uses fewer than the pinned workers; or
- a candidate passes only by lowering security or expanding product scope.
